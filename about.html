About Woobee

Built for the commute. Made for the climb.

We make backpacks and travel carry for people who live two lives a week, one in the city and one on the trail. Good gear should make both easier, more comfortable and a little more meaningful.

Read the story

Our story

Woobee started with a simple frustration. The bag that looked right on the train fell apart on the trail, and the one that survived the trail looked wrong everywhere else.

So a small group of hikers and designers set out to make one bag for both lives: sharp enough for the café, tough enough for the ridge, and organised enough that you stop thinking about it.

Every Woobee since is designed for both days of your week. Same canvas, same hardware, same idea.

Our philosophy

More than just backpacks.

We design for the journey and for the moments along the way. Where you go matters. How you feel getting there matters more.

That's why the details come first: a sleeve that finds your laptop without looking, a strap that sits right after ten kilometres, a pocket for the thing you always need.

Sustainability

Built for a bigger tomorrow.

The most sustainable bag is the one you never have to replace. So we design for years, not seasons: hard-wearing canvas, hardware that takes a beating, and shapes that won't date by next spring.

Buy it once, use it for everything, keep it for a long time.
